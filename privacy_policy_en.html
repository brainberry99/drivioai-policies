Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 7, 2026

1. Purposes of Processing Personal Information

Drivio AI (hereinafter "the Service") processes personal information for the purposes described below. Personal information processed by the Service will not be used for any purposes other than those stated unless required or permitted by law.

1.1 Registration and Account Management

  • To verify a user's intent to register and to identify and authenticate members for membership-based services
  • To maintain and manage membership status and to prevent fraudulent or unauthorized use of services
  • To provide notices and communications and to handle complaints and inquiries

1.2 Provision of Goods or Services

  • To provide vehicle management services and AI diagnostic services
  • To provide premium subscription services
  • To provide personalized services

2. Categories of Personal Information Processed

The Service processes the following categories of personal information.

2.1 Mandatory Information

  • When using Kakao login: Kakao account information (unique ID)
  • When using Apple login: Apple ID (unique ID), email (optional)

2.2 Optional Information

  • Vehicle information (model name, nickname, mileage, etc.)
  • Vehicle photos (images taken and uploaded by the user)
  • Vehicle maintenance records (fueling records, service records, etc.)

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Service usage logs and access records
  • Device information (device model, OS version)

3. Retention and Use Period of Personal Information

The Service retains and uses personal information within the period required by applicable law or the retention period agreed to when the personal information was collected from the data subject.

3.1 Member Information

  • Retention period: Until the member withdraws
  • Upon member withdrawal, personal information will be destroyed immediately, except where retention is required by law

3.2 Vehicle Management Records

  • Retention period: Until the member withdraws
  • If the user requests deletion, records will be destroyed without delay
⚠️ Important: Where preservation is required by law, the Service retains the information for the period specified by relevant laws.

4. Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

The Service does not, in principle, provide users' personal information to third parties. Exceptions include:

  • When the user has given prior consent
  • When required by law or when a lawfully authorized investigative agency requests disclosure for investigative purposes in accordance with legal procedures

5. Entrustment of Personal Information Processing

To provide smooth service, the Service entrusts the following personal information processing tasks to third-party providers.

5.1 Service Providers and Processing Tasks

  • Firebase (Google LLC): Authentication, data storage, analytics
  • Kakao: Kakao login
  • Apple: Apple login
  • Google Gemini AI: Image analysis and AI diagnostics

6. Rights and Obligations of Data Subjects and How to Exercise Them

As a data subject, you have the following rights:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Request restriction of processing

You can exercise these rights through the app's settings menu or by contacting the Personal Data Protection Officer in writing, by phone, or by email.

7. Destruction of Personal Information

7.1 Destruction Procedure

Personal information provided by users will be moved to a separate database (or printed material will be stored separately) after the purpose of collection is achieved, and will be destroyed after a certain retention period in accordance with internal procedures and related laws.

7.2 Destruction Methods

  • Electronic files: Permanently deleted so that records cannot be restored
  • Paper documents: Shredded or incinerated
